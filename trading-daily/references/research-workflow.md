# Morning Research Workflow

Follow this workflow sequentially.

Do not jump directly to stock selection.

# Phase 1 — Establish Market Context

Determine:

- current date
- current time in Asia/Jakarta
- whether IDX is expected to trade today
- major market events scheduled today

Relevant scheduled events may include:

- Bank Indonesia decisions
- Federal Reserve decisions
- inflation releases
- employment data
- GDP releases
- major commodity events
- Indonesian government announcements
- important corporate actions

Only include events relevant to today's market.

---

# Phase 2 — Overnight Global Scan

Review developments after the previous Indonesian market session.

Evaluate:

## US Markets

Look at:

- S&P 500
- Nasdaq
- Dow Jones when relevant

Do not simply report whether indexes were green or red.

Identify why markets moved.

Possible drivers:

- rates
- inflation
- earnings
- AI / technology
- geopolitics
- economic data
- policy announcements

Ask:

"Does this development plausibly affect Indonesian equities today?"

If no:

omit it.

---

## Rates and Currency

Evaluate when available:

- US Treasury yields
- US Dollar strength
- USD/IDR
- relevant Asian currencies

Pay particular attention when currency moves could affect:

- import-heavy businesses
- companies with USD debt
- exporters
- commodity producers
- foreign capital flows

---

# Phase 3 — Commodity Scan

Review commodities with meaningful IDX exposure.

Examples:

- crude oil
- coal
- nickel
- gold
- copper
- CPO
- natural gas when relevant

Do not include every commodity every day.

Only report meaningful developments.

For each important commodity move determine:

commodity move
→ Indonesian sector
→ exposed companies
→ expected directional impact

Example:

Nickel price rises materially

Possible impact:

positive:
nickel miners with direct exposure

mixed:
downstream processors depending on margins and contracts

Do not automatically assume every miner benefits equally.

---

# Phase 4 — Indonesia News Scan

Search for material Indonesian developments.

Prioritize:

## Company Events

- earnings
- guidance
- contracts
- acquisitions
- divestments
- IPO-related developments
- rights issues
- buybacks
- dividends
- debt refinancing
- credit rating changes
- production updates
- management changes
- legal or regulatory actions

## Macro

- Bank Indonesia
- inflation
- trade balance
- GDP
- fiscal policy
- government spending
- rupiah
- foreign flows

## Regulation

- OJK
- IDX
- ministries
- tax policy
- export regulations
- import regulations
- commodity policy

---

# Phase 5 — Cluster News

Do not treat ten articles about one announcement as ten independent catalysts.

Group related reporting into one event.

For each event record:

EVENT

WHAT HAPPENED

WHEN

PRIMARY SOURCE

INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION

AFFECTED SECTOR

AFFECTED COMPANIES

LIKELY DIRECTION

UNCERTAINTIES

---

# Phase 6 — Identify Candidate Stocks

Generate an initial candidate pool.

A company enters the candidate pool only when there is a plausible reason the event affects:

- revenue
- costs
- margins
- volumes
- valuation
- balance sheet
- regulation
- capital allocation
- market sentiment

Avoid purely associative reasoning.

Bad reasoning:

"Gold is in the news, therefore every gold stock is a buy."

Better reasoning:

"Gold rose materially after X catalyst, company Y derives substantial earnings exposure from gold, and the move has not obviously been reflected in its latest market price."

---

# Phase 7 — Check Market Context

For shortlisted candidates attempt to verify:

- latest reliable price
- previous close
- recent price trend
- recent volume
- whether catalyst has already caused a large move
- unusual gap or spike
- major nearby support/resistance when reliable data permits

Technical analysis is secondary.

News and market data must never be fabricated.

If reliable current market data cannot be retrieved:

state:

"Current price context could not be reliably verified."

Continue using verified fundamental/news evidence only.

---

# Phase 8 — Contradiction Search

Before recommending a candidate actively look for reasons the thesis may be wrong.

Search for:

- conflicting news
- negative company announcements
- valuation concerns
- regulatory risks
- commodity reversal
- currency risk
- broad market risk
- catalyst already priced in

This phase is mandatory.

Do not search only for evidence supporting the initial thesis.
