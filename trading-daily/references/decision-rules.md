# Trading Decision Rules

Every shortlisted stock must receive exactly one classification:

CONSIDER BUY
WATCH
WAIT
AVOID

These are research classifications, not trade execution instructions.

# CONSIDER BUY

Use only when all conditions are satisfied:

- catalyst is recent
- catalyst is verified
- company exposure is reasonably direct
- source quality is high
- expected financial or sentiment transmission mechanism is clear
- no major contradictory information invalidates the thesis
- stock liquidity is acceptable
- available market context does not strongly suggest the opportunity is already exhausted

The report must include:

Catalyst
Thesis
Risk
Invalidation

CONSIDER BUY does not mean guaranteed upside.

---

# WATCH

Use when the thesis is interesting but one important element is missing.

Examples:

- catalyst is strong but market reaction needs confirmation
- source is credible but primary confirmation is unavailable
- commodity move is relevant but company sensitivity is unclear
- stock already moved strongly
- broader market conditions are uncertain

WATCH means:

"Interesting enough to monitor, but evidence is not sufficient for a stronger classification."

---

# WAIT

Use when:

- thesis may become interesting later
- current timing is poor
- catalyst is already heavily priced
- important data is expected soon
- risk/reward cannot currently be assessed
- current price data cannot be verified and timing is essential

---

# AVOID

Use when:

- information is primarily rumor
- catalyst is contradicted
- stock is excessively speculative or illiquid
- narrative does not match company fundamentals
- downside risk materially outweighs current evidence
- suspicious price action exists without reliable catalyst

AVOID does not necessarily mean:

"the company is bad."

It means:

"this morning's setup does not meet the evidence standard."

---

# Primary Candidate

A Primary Candidate may only be selected from stocks classified:

CONSIDER BUY

If no stock meets that classification:

Primary Candidate: NONE

Never promote a WATCH stock merely because the template expects a primary candidate.

---

# Confidence

Use:

HIGH

when:
- sources are authoritative
- catalyst is confirmed
- transmission mechanism is direct
- contradictions are limited

MEDIUM

when:
- thesis is reasonable
- some uncertainty remains

LOW

when:
- evidence is incomplete
- timing is uncertain
- important data could not be verified

Low-confidence research should rarely produce CONSIDER BUY.

---

# Anti-FOMO Rule

Never upgrade a candidate because:

- it is trending
- many people are discussing it
- price recently surged
- influencers mention it

Popularity is not evidence of expected return.

---

# No-Trade Outcome

Always allow:

Primary Candidate: NONE

A no-trade morning is a valid analytical outcome.

Do not manufacture opportunity.
