# Candidate Scoring Framework

Use this framework to compare candidate stocks consistently.

The score is an internal decision aid.

Do not show raw scoring unless explicitly requested.

Evaluate each category from 0 to 5.

# A. Catalyst Strength

0:
No identifiable catalyst.

1:
Weak narrative or old information.

2:
Real development but indirect impact.

3:
Relevant catalyst with plausible financial impact.

4:
Strong recent catalyst with direct business impact.

5:
Exceptional catalyst with clearly material direct impact.

---

# B. Source Confidence

0:
Unverified rumor.

1:
Single weak secondary source.

2:
Credible secondary reporting but no primary confirmation.

3:
Multiple credible sources.

4:
Primary source confirmed.

5:
Primary source plus strong independent confirmation.

---

# C. Company Exposure

0:
Little or no relationship to catalyst.

1:
Very indirect exposure.

2:
Some exposure.

3:
Meaningful business exposure.

4:
Large direct exposure.

5:
Catalyst directly affects a major earnings driver.

---

# D. Freshness

0:
Old and already widely known.

1:
Mostly stale.

2:
Recent but likely digested.

3:
Fresh within the relevant market window.

4:
Very recent.

5:
New information emerging immediately before today's session.

---

# E. Market Opportunity

Evaluate whether the expected effect may still matter to the current price.

0:
Clearly stale or fully reflected.

1:
Price has already moved extremely aggressively.

2:
Opportunity appears limited.

3:
Reasonable remaining opportunity.

4:
Strong setup with limited evidence of full pricing.

5:
Very strong fresh setup.

If reliable current market data is unavailable:

maximum score for this category is 3.

---

# F. Liquidity

0:
Highly illiquid/speculative.

1:
Poor liquidity.

2:
Below preferred liquidity.

3:
Acceptable.

4:
Good.

5:
Highly liquid major name.

Favor liquidity for morning recommendations.

---

# G. Risk Penalty

Evaluate downside uncertainty.

0:
Low unusual risk.

1:
Minor risks.

2:
Moderate uncertainty.

3:
Meaningful uncertainty.

4:
High risk.

5:
Extreme uncertainty or major unresolved contradiction.

This score is SUBTRACTED.

---

# Total

Calculate conceptually:

Catalyst Strength
+ Source Confidence
+ Company Exposure
+ Freshness
+ Market Opportunity
+ Liquidity
- Risk Penalty

Maximum theoretical score:

30

Use score as a comparison tool, not as mathematical truth.

Never let score override obvious contradictory evidence.

---

# Interpretation

24-30:
Strong candidate for CONSIDER BUY evaluation.

19-23:
Usually WATCH.
May become CONSIDER BUY only when evidence quality is particularly strong.

14-18:
WATCH or WAIT.

Below 14:
Normally WAIT or AVOID.

These thresholds are guidance.

The final classification must also satisfy decision-rules.md.
