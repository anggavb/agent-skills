# Formatting Rules

Keep explanations concise.

Do not repeat the same information in multiple sections.

Do not provide lengthy disclaimers.

Do not include raw scoring.

Do not expose research scratchpad.

Maximum actionable candidates:

3

Preferred actionable candidates:

1-2

When no candidate qualifies:

Primary: NONE

and explain why briefly.
