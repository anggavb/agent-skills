# Source Policy

The quality of the morning brief depends on source quality.

Follow this hierarchy.

# Tier 1 — Primary Sources

Prefer these whenever available.

Examples:

- Indonesia Stock Exchange / IDX announcements
- company filings
- issuer investor relations
- Bank Indonesia
- OJK
- Ministry of Finance
- relevant Indonesian ministries
- BPS
- official government publications
- official company announcements
- official exchange disclosures

Use Tier 1 to verify material company-specific claims whenever possible.

---

# Tier 2 — High Quality Financial News

Examples:

- Reuters
- Bloomberg
- Financial Times
- CNBC when reporting original financial news
- major established financial news organizations

Use these for:

- breaking events
- market reaction
- global macro
- commodities
- cross-market context

---

# Tier 3 — Established Indonesian Financial Media

Use reputable Indonesian financial publications for:

- local company news
- sector developments
- government policy
- domestic market sentiment

Whenever a material claim originates here:

attempt to find the corresponding primary source.

---

# Tier 4 — Secondary Sources

Examples:

- aggregators
- blogs
- commentary
- analyst summaries
- social media

These may be useful for discovery.

They should not normally be the sole basis of a CONSIDER BUY classification.

---

# Social Media

Social media can identify topics receiving attention.

It cannot by itself verify:

- earnings
- contracts
- corporate actions
- government regulations
- insider information
- financial results

Treat viral claims as unverified until confirmed.

Never recommend a stock solely because it is trending.

---

# Confirmation Requirement

For a material corporate catalyst:

prefer:

PRIMARY SOURCE
+
at least one credible independent report

When a primary source exists, treat it as the factual anchor.

For broad macro events:

one authoritative primary source may be sufficient.

Example:

Bank Indonesia interest-rate announcement.

---

# Publication Time

Always inspect publication time when available.

Avoid accidentally treating:

yesterday's article

as:

today's new catalyst.

When an older article receives renewed attention:

identify why it matters today.

---

# Duplicate Reporting

Several publications may repeat the same wire report.

Do not treat them as independent confirmation.

Try to determine the original source.

---

# Conflicting Sources

When credible sources disagree:

do not silently select the preferred version.

State:

"Sources disagree on..."

Then explain what is confirmed and what remains uncertain.

---

# Source Citations

For each major story retain:

- publisher
- headline or topic
- publication time when available
- URL when available

The Telegram report does not need dozens of links.

Prefer approximately 3-8 high-value sources.

Quality is more important than quantity.
